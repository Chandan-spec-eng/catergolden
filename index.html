About Us

CATERGOLDEN SERVICES LLP is a Kolkata-based premium catering company specialising in Corporate Catering, Industrial Canteen Management, Executive Meal Boxes, Wedding Catering, Pantry Services and Event Management. We believe that great food and professional service create memorable experiences. Every meal is prepared with fresh ingredients, strict hygiene standards and timely delivery to ensure complete customer satisfaction.
